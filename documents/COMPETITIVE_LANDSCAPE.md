## Competitive Gaps PaidToBe Fills

1. **Career risk + relocation intelligence combined.** No platform answers "my job is at risk — where in the world should I move?"
2. **Country-level AI policy comparison.** Nobody ranks countries by AI regulation, UBI programs, worker protections, and retraining investment for individual decision-making.
3. **Personalized "AI-proof career + location" recommendations.** Risk calculators tell you a score. Relocation tools tell you cost of living. Nobody gives you an integrated plan.
4. **Dynamic intelligence for the AI transition.** All risk calculators are static snapshots. Nobody offers ongoing intelligence tracking AI impacts on specific jobs in specific countries.
5. **UBI and safety nets as a relocation factor.** No platform treats government support programs as part of the "where to live" equation.
6. **Affordable self-serve intelligence ($10–$50/mo).** The market jumps from free tools to $2,000+ consulting. Nothing in between.
