## The Three Axes

PaidToBe operates across three intersecting dimensions of the post-labor transition:

```
                    IDENTITY AXIS
                   "Who am I becoming?"
                         ↑
                         |
                         |
        JOB AXIS ────────┼──────── LOCATION AXIS
    "Is my career safe?"  |     "Where should I live?"
                         |
                         |
                    (Rehabit.ai
                     overlap zone)
```

### Axis 1: Job — "Optimize My Career"
- 341+ occupations scored for AI displacement risk
- Median pay, growth outlook, task-level automation analysis
- Career pivot pathways, skill adaptation strategies
- **Entry question:** "Will AI take my job?"

### Axis 2: Location — "Optimize My Place"
- 28+ countries (scaling to 195+) scored on post-labor readiness
- UBI status, safety net strength, visa accessibility, cost of thriving
- Policy tracking, prediction markets, immigration pathways
- **Entry question:** "Where should I live?"

### Axis 3: Identity — "Optimize My Self"
- Purpose and identity transformation during career disruption
- Reframing professional identity beyond job titles
- Building resilience, new habits, and new narratives
- **Entry question:** "Who am I if I'm not my job?"
- **Note:** This axis overlaps with Rehabit.ai — see integration notes below

## Revenue Architecture

### How the funnel works:

```
SEO / Social / Viral Risk Scores
         ↓
    FREE TOOLS (95-97% stay here)
         ↓
    Email Capture (15-25% of visitors)
         ↓
    Newsletter (weekly intelligence)
         ↓
    ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
    │  CONVERSION TRIGGERS:           │
    │  • Crossover discovery          │
    │    (Job → Country or reverse)   │
    │  • Real-world event             │
    │    (layoff, policy change)      │
    │  • Personalization need         │
    │    ("What about MY situation?") │
    └─────────────────────────────────┘
         ↓                    ↓
    Navigator ($19/mo)   Info Product ($49-$499)
         ↓                    ↓
    Strategist ($49/mo)  Premium Report ($199-$499)
         ↓
    Enterprise ($5K-$50K/yr)
```

### Revenue mix targets (at scale):

| Stream | % of Revenue | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Subscriptions (Navigator + Strategist) | 50-60% | Predictable recurring |
| Info Products (courses + reports) | 20-30% | High margin, no churn |
| Enterprise / API | 10-20% | High LTV, long sales cycle |
| Affiliate / Partnerships | 5-10% | Immigration services, relocation providers |

## Rehabit.ai Integration Notes

The Identity Axis represents a natural bridge between PaidToBe and Rehabit.ai:

**PaidToBe diagnoses:** Your job has a 78% AI risk score. Finland has the strongest safety net. Here's your relocation timeline.

**Rehabit helps transform:** You're not "a copywriter whose career is dying." You're someone building a new relationship with work, purpose, and contribution. Here's how to navigate that inner transition.

### Potential Integration Points

1. **Cross-promotion:** PaidToBe career risk results → "Struggling with what this means for your identity? Rehabit can help."
2. **Bundled product:** "Career Transition Bundle" combining PaidToBe's Migration Report + Rehabit's identity transformation program
3. **Shared courses:** The "Who Am I Now?" workshop could live on either platform or both
4. **Data feedback loop:** Rehabit's habit/identity data could inform PaidToBe's understanding of what makes career transitions successful
5. **Unified account:** Eventually, one login across both platforms

### What NOT to do yet
- Don't merge the platforms — they serve different buying moments
- Don't force the Rehabit angle on users who just want data — the Identity axis is opt-in
- Don't build the integration before both platforms have independent traction
